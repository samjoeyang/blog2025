<p>想确定你的Gmail是否安全？请访问：<a href="http://mail.google.com/support/bin/static.py?page=checklist.cs&amp;tab=29488">http://mail.google.com/support/bin/static.py?page=checklist.cs&amp;tab=29488</a></p>
<p>Gmail的支持页面里<a href="http://mail.google.com/support/bin/static.py?page=checklist.cs&amp;tab=29488">更新了一篇安全清单</a>，通过以下5个方法即可检查自己的Gmail是否有安全隐患：</p>
<ol>
<li><a href="https://www.google.com/accounts/IssuedAuthSubTokens">在这里</a>查看授权获取Google帐户数据的网站。我自认为自己的安全功夫做的很到家，但在这里依然发现了1个奇怪的网站获得了Google帐户的访问权，赶紧ban掉</li>
<li>检查你浏览器的插件、扩展和第三方应用是否需要访问你的Google帐户。比如你安装了一个可检查Gmail新邮件的扩展，那么Google是无法控制这个扩展的安全性的，如果你安装了并不知名的扩展，需要小心了</li>
<li>查看邮件设置，以确保你的Gmail邮件没有被转发出去，有四个地方的设置会有安全隐患：
<ul>
<li><strong>常规</strong>：检查签名、外出回复</li>
<li><strong>帐户</strong>：检查'用这个地址发送邮件'、</li>
<li><strong>过滤器</strong>：检查是否有过滤器将你的邮件转发到垃圾箱、垃圾邮件，或是转发到其它邮箱里</li>
<li><strong>转发和POP/IMAP</strong>：查看这里是否有未知的帐户或邮件客户端</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>检查帐户最近的可疑活动。点击Gmail最下面的'上次帐户活动时间'后的'详细信息'链接，看看是否有可疑的IP访问了你的Gmail</li>
<li>进入设置的'常规'里，将'浏览器连接'设置为'始终使用 https'，以确保当你使用咖啡厅或酒店的公众网络访问邮箱时不会泄露信息。</li>
</ol>
<p>原文：<br />
Gmail's support site has <a href="http://mail.google.com/support/bin/static.py?page=checklist.cs&amp;tab=29488">a security checklist</a><br />
that's useful if you want to make sure that your Gmail account is<br />
secure. There are some obvious tips like updating your operating system<br />
and your browser, but Google also posted some advanced tricks:</p>
<p>1. '<span style="font-weight: bold;">Check the list of websites that are authorized to access your Google Account data.</span><br />
Make sure that the list of authorized websites are accurate and ones<br />
that you have chosen. If your Google Account has been compromised<br />
recently, it's possible that the bad guys could have authorized their<br />
own websites to access your account data.' To edit the list of<br />
authorized websites, go to <a href="https://www.google.com/accounts/IssuedAuthSubTokens">this page</a>.</p>
<p>2. '<span style="font-weight: bold;">Check<br />
your browser for plug-ins, extensions, and third-party programs/tools<br />
that require access to your Google Account credentials.</span> Plug-ins<br />
and extensions are downloadable computer programs that work with your<br />
browser to perform specific tasks. For example, you may have downloaded a<br />
plug-in or extension that checks your Gmail inbox for new messages.<br />
Google can't guarantee the security of these third party services. If<br />
those services are compromised, so is your Gmail password.'</p>
<p>3.<br />
'Confirm the accuracy of your mail settings to ensure that your mail<br />
stays and goes where you want it to. Sign in to your account and click<br />
on the Settings link at the top to check the following tabs:</p>
<p>* <span style="font-weight: bold;">General</span>: Check Signature, Vacation Responder, and/or canned responses for spammy content<br />
* <span style="font-weight: bold;">Accounts</span>: Verify your Send Mail As, Get mail from other accounts, and Grant access to your account are all accurate.<br />
* <span style="font-weight: bold;">Filters</span>: Check that no filters are sending your mail to Trash, Spam, or forwarding to an unknown account.<br />
* <span style="font-weight: bold;">Forwarding and POP/IMAP</span>: Ensure your mail isn't sent to an unknown account or mail client.'</p>
<p>4. '<span style="font-weight: bold;">Check for any strange recent activity on your account.</span><br />
Click the Details link next to the 'Last Account Activity' entry at the<br />
bottom of your account to see the time, date, IP address and the<br />
associated location of recent access to your account.'</p>
<p>5. '<span style="font-weight: bold;">Use a secure connection to sign in.</span><br />
In your Gmail settings, select 'Always use HTTPS.' This setting<br />
protects your information from being stolen when you're signing in to<br />
Gmail on a public wireless network, like at a cafe or hotel.'</p>
