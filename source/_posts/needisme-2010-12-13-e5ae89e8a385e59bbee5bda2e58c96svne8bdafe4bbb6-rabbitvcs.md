<p>1. Go to http://wiki.rabbitvcs.org/wiki/download and click on the PPA link<br />
2. Add &ldquo;deb http://ppa.launchpad.net/rabbitvcs/ppa/ubuntu karmic main&rdquo; to<br />
/etc/apt/sources.list as requested<br />
3. sudo apt-key adv &ndash;keyserver keyserver.ubuntu.com &ndash;recv-keys 34EF4A35<br />
4. sudo apt-get update<br />
5. sudo apt-get install rabbitvcs-cli  rabbitvcs-core rabbitvcs-gedit rabbitvcs-nautilus rabbitvcs-thunar thunarx-python</p>
